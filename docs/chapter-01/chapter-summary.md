# 1.4 Chapter Summary

## Key Concepts Review

### Embodied Cognition
- Intelligence emerges from physical interaction
- Cognition is situated and grounded in experience
- Traditional AI assumptions break down in physical systems

### Sensorimotor Integration
- Closed-loop perception-action cycles
- Real-time coordination requirements
- Neural models of sensorimotor processing

### Physical Constraints
- Computational, mechanical, and environmental limitations
- Design trade-offs required for physical implementation
- Safety and reliability as fundamental requirements

## Chapter Relationships

### Connections to Chapter 2 (ROS 2)
- Real-time processing requirements drive middleware design
- Sensorimotor integration informs node communication patterns
- Physical constraints shape QoS policy decisions

### Preview of Subsequent Chapters
- Chapter 3: Simulation as a tool for understanding physical constraints
- Chapter 4: AI acceleration to overcome computational limitations
- Chapter 5: Autonomous systems integrating all embodied principles
