# Glossary

## A

**Action Interface**: ROS 2 communication pattern for long-running operations with feedback and cancellation capabilities.

**Actuator**: Mechanical component that converts control signals into physical motion or force.

## B

**Base Link**: Reference coordinate frame in ROS 2 representing the robot's base or root link.

## C

**Closed-Loop Control**: Control system that uses feedback from sensors to adjust actuator commands.

**Computational Graph**: Data structure representing computational operations and their dependencies in neural networks.

## D

**DDS (Data Distribution Service)**: Real-time publish-subscribe middleware underlying ROS 2 communications.

**Digital Twin**: Virtual representation of a physical system that mirrors its structure, behavior, and context.

## E

**Embodied Cognition**: Theory that intelligence emerges from dynamic interaction between agent, body, and environment.

**End-Effector**: Terminal component of a robotic manipulator (e.g., gripper, tool).

## F

**Forward Kinematics**: Mathematical computation of end-effector pose given joint angles.

## G

**Gazebo**: Physics-based simulation environment integrated with ROS 2.

## H

**Humanoid Robot**: Robot designed to mimic human form and capabilities.

## I

**Inverse Kinematics**: Computation of joint angles required to achieve desired end-effector pose.

**Isaac Sim**: NVIDIA's robotics simulation platform with AI acceleration capabilities.

## J

**Joint**: Movable connection between rigid links in a robotic mechanism.

## K

**Kinematic Chain**: Sequence of links and joints connecting base to end-effector.

## L

**Latency**: Time delay between stimulus and response in a system.

**Link**: Rigid component in a robotic mechanism between joints.

## M

**Middleware**: Software layer providing communication and coordination services between distributed components.

## N

**Node**: Executable process in ROS 2 that performs computation and communicates via topics, services, and actions.

## O

**Open-Loop Control**: Control system that generates commands without feedback from sensors.

## P

**Perception Pipeline**: Sequence of processing steps converting raw sensor data into actionable information.

**Proprioception**: Sense of body position and movement from internal sensors.

## Q

**QoS (Quality of Service)**: ROS 2 policies controlling communication reliability, durability, and timing.

## R

**Reality Gap**: Difference between simulated and real-world system behavior.

**ROS 2 (Robot Operating System 2)**: Real-time robotic middleware framework.

## S

**Sensor Fusion**: Integration of data from multiple sensors to improve accuracy and robustness.

**Sensorimotor Loop**: Closed cycle of sensory input, processing, motor output, and environmental feedback.

## T

**Topic**: Named communication channel in ROS 2 for publish-subscribe messaging.

**Trajectory**: Time-parameterized path specifying position, velocity, and acceleration.

## U

**URDF (Unified Robot Description Format)**: XML format for describing robot kinematics and dynamics.

## V

**Vision-Language-Action**: Integrated system combining visual perception, language understanding, and physical action.

## W

**World Model**: Internal representation of the environment maintained by an intelligent system.
