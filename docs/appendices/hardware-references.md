# Hardware References

## Robotic Platforms

### Humanoid Robots

#### Boston Dynamics Atlas
- **Degrees of Freedom**: 28
- **Height**: 188 cm
- **Weight**: 80 kg
- **Key Capabilities**: Bipedal locomotion, manipulation, rough terrain navigation

#### Toyota Human Support Robot
- **Degrees of Freedom**: 35
- **Height**: 160 cm
- **Weight**: 60 kg
- **Key Capabilities**: Human assistance, object manipulation, safe interaction

#### PAL Robotics TALOS
- **Degrees of Freedom**: 32
- **Height**: 174 cm
- **Weight**: 100 kg
- **Key Capabilities**: Research platform, ROS integration, payload capacity

## Sensors

### Vision Systems
- **RGB-D Cameras**: Intel RealSense, Microsoft Kinect
- **Stereo Cameras**: ZED 2, Bumblebee XB3
- **Thermal Cameras**: FLIR Lepton, Seek Thermal

### Inertial Measurement Units
- **High-precision**: Bosch BMI088, ST ISM330DHCX
- **Industrial-grade**: Honeywell HG4930, Northrop Grumman LN-200

### Force/Torque Sensors
- **6-axis F/T**: ATI Industrial Automation, JR3
- **Tactile sensors**: SynTouch BioTac, Pressure Profile Systems

## Computing Platforms

### Edge Computing
- **NVIDIA Jetson Series**: Xavier NX, Orin Nano, AGX Orin
- **Intel Neural Compute Stick**: NCS 2, Compute Stick
- **Google Coral**: TPU-based acceleration

### Development Workstations
- **Recommended Specs**: Intel i7/AMD Ryzen 7, 32GB RAM, NVIDIA RTX 30-series
- **Storage**: 500GB NVMe SSD minimum
- **Network**: Gigabit Ethernet for ROS 2 communication

## Power Systems

### Battery Technologies
- **Lithium-ion**: High energy density, 3.7V nominal
- **Lithium-polymer**: Flexible form factor, higher discharge rates
- **Safety Considerations**: BMS integration, thermal management

### Power Management
- **DC-DC Converters**: Efficient voltage regulation
- **Power Distribution**: Protected circuits, current monitoring
- **Energy Harvesting**: Solar, kinetic, thermal options

## Networking

### Real-time Communication
- **Ethernet**: Deterministic latency for ROS 2
- **Wi-Fi 6**: High bandwidth for sensor data
- **5G**: Low-latency cellular for mobile robots

### Industrial Protocols
- **EtherCAT**: Real-time fieldbus for servo control
- **CAN bus**: Automotive-grade communication
- **Modbus**: Industrial automation integration
